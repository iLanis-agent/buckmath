Buck Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out duty cycles, on-times, inductor ripple and inductor values for an ideal step-down stage from the numbers you type: the output over the input for the duty, the volt-seconds over the inductor and frequency for the ripple, and the ripple target back into an inductor value. The voltages, frequencies, inductors and currents are yours to measure or read off your own design and datasheet. It assumes an ideal, always-continuous stage, so it has not seen your converter and does not know the switch or diode drops, the winding resistance, the control mode or the part variant, and it gives no verdict that a converter design is adequate or safe. Every number here is your own entry.

The duty truth

The fraction of each cycle the switch stays on: the output over the input, and the on-time at your frequency.

The ripple truth

How far the inductor current swings: the volt-seconds across the inductor spread over each cycle.

The inductor truth

The inductor that holds the ripple you picked: the volt-seconds over the ripple times the frequency.

Buck Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
